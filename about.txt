Who We Are
SuperAI Community – Stakeholder Connect is a student-led community of the Department of Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning at TCET, established in 2022 with purpose to create a platform where students can learn beyond the curriculum, build meaningful skills, and grow together as a community.
What began as a department-level initiative has grown into a collaborative ecosystem built around students, faculty, alumni, and industry professionals.
SuperAI aims to bridge the gap between classroom learning and the rapidly evolving world of Artificial Intelligence by creating opportunities for students to learn from one another, interact with professionals, explore emerging technologies, participate in challenges, and turn knowledge into practical experience.
Our long-term vision is to grow into one of the most impactful student-led AI communities, creating an environment where curiosity leads to learning, learning leads to building, and building leads to innovation and leadership.
Our Philosophy

📚 Learn
Go beyond the prescribed curriculum through peer learning, workshops, expert sessions, and continuous exploration.
🛠️ Build
Transform knowledge into action by developing projects, solving problems, experimenting with technologies, and gaining hands-on experience.
💡 Innovate
Think beyond conventional solutions, participate in challenges, and use technology to address meaningful problems.
🚀 Lead
Take initiative, accept responsibility, collaborate with others, and develop the confidence to lead both within and beyond the community.
Our Stakeholder Connect
SuperAI is built around one central idea:
Students ↔ Faculty ↔ Alumni ↔ Industry
We bring these stakeholders together to create a community where knowledge and experience flow beyond traditional classroom boundaries.
Students bring curiosity, ideas, creativity, and the drive to explore.
Faculty provide mentorship, academic guidance, and direction.
Alumni bring real-world experiences, career insights, and mentorship.
Industry professionals provide exposure to current technologies, industry expectations, practical applications, and emerging opportunities.
Together, they create an ecosystem where students can learn from experience, connect with people, and prepare for the future.
What Makes SuperAI Different?
Peer Learning
Our strongest identity is our culture of students learning from students.
SuperAI encourages members to share knowledge, teach peers, collaborate on problems, and grow together. Learning isn't restricted to a classroom or a speaker on a stage — every member can become both a learner and a contributor.
Industry Exposure
Through expert sessions, alumni interactions, workshops, and industry-oriented activities, students get a glimpse of how AI and emerging technologies are actually applied beyond academics.
AI-Focused Community
SuperAI provides a dedicated environment for students passionate about Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Generative AI, emerging technologies, research, and innovation.
Learn Beyond Classrooms
We believe that the classroom provides the foundation — but projects, competitions, collaboration, experimentation, and real-world exposure complete the learning experience.

Our Journey
2022 — The Beginning
SuperAI was established to create a platform for AI/ML students to learn beyond the academic curriculum and build a strong AI community within the college.
Growing the Community
The community expanded its activities through peer learning, coding competitions, AI/ML competitions, expert interactions, and technical sessions.
Expanding Exposure
SuperAI introduced students to emerging areas such as Deep Learning, Generative AI, MLOps, Explainable AI, AI tools, and industry applications, alongside career and higher-study guidance.
Today
SuperAI continues to evolve as a student-led ecosystem connecting students, faculty, alumni, and industry, with a vision to create a larger and more impactful AI community.
